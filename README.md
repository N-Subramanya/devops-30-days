# 📅 DevOps 30 Days

A 30-day, learn-in-public DevOps series. Each day has one topic, a diagram or flow to draw, and a small hands-on build you can reuse at work.

## How each day is structured

| Section | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Concept** | The idea in plain words, and why it matters in production |
| **Draw** | A flow or diagram to sketch (rendered with Mermaid on GitHub) |
| **Build** | A small, practical deliverable you can copy and adapt |
| **Checklist** | What "done" looks like for the day |
